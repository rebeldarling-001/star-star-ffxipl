CHAPTER 01
General Rules
1

The admin decision is final. Tournament admins reserve the right to take any decision — on gameplay, penalties, disputes or eligibility — as they see fit, and no further discussion will be entertained once a ruling is made.

2

All 8 franchises must field a minimum of 4 active players and 1 substitute for every scheduled match.

3

Rosters lock 48 hours before the opening match. No trades, loans or swaps once the season starts.

4

Every player must check in on the official Discord 30 minutes before their lobby opens.

5

Matches run on the latest stable Free Fire client. Outdated builds are not eligible to queue.

6

Emulator play is strictly banned. Mobile devices only, verified via in-lobby screen share.

🗺️
CHAPTER 02
Match Format
1

Season 11 runs a 12-match round robin followed by a 4-team knockout final.

2

Maps rotate Bermuda → Purgatory → Kalahari across each round in a fixed sequence.

3

Lobbies open on a strict 15-minute countdown. Late squads forfeit that match's placement points.

4

A verified admin spectates every lobby. All matches are recorded for review.

5

Server disconnects mid-match are eligible for a re-queue only if reported within 5 minutes, with admin footage confirming the drop.

🎯
CHAPTER 03
Scoring System
1

Placement points follow the standard curve: #1 = 15 pts, scaling down to 1 pt for 11th–12th.

2

Every confirmed elimination is worth 1 point, credited to the player who lands the kill.

3

Booyah bonus adds a flat +10 points to the winning squad's match total.

4

Standings are decided by total points; ties are broken by total eliminations, then by most Booyahs.

🤝
CHAPTER 04
Player Conduct
1

Zero tolerance for hacks, macros, aimbots, or any third-party software that alters gameplay.

2

Teaming with rival squads to eliminate a common opponent is forbidden in every phase.

3

Harassment, hate speech or toxicity in voice or text chat is grounds for immediate review.

4

Stream sniping — using a live broadcast to gain positional intel — results in an automatic forfeit.

5

Smurfing on unregistered accounts is banned. Every player competes under their verified ID only.

🚨
Zero-Tolerance NoticeConfirmed cheating results in an instant season-long ban for the player and a points deduction for the whole squad.

CHAPTER 06
Disputes & Appeals
1

Disputes must be filed via the official Discord within 1 hour of the match ending.

2

The admin panel reviews recorded footage and rules within 24 hours. Their decision is final and binding — admins may take any action necessary to keep the tournament fair, including outcomes not explicitly listed in this rulebook.

3

Franchises may appeal a ban once per season with supporting evidence, reviewed by a neutral panel.
